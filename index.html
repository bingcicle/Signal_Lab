UNDER THE HOOD

The numbers, explained.

Signal Lab is a coding demo with generated market data. Its returns describe a synthetic scenario, not real trading performance.

  1. Reproducible markets. A seeded generator creates 720 hourly bars. A new scenario changes the seed for every market.
  2. Causal execution. Strategies read completed bars. Signals execute at the next bar’s open, with adverse slippage.
  3. Transparent accounting. One long or short position at a time. Notional is capped at your setting and available equity. Fees apply to both sides.
  4. Fair comparison. Buy & hold uses the same capital, initial position cap, costs, and next-open execution convention. It enters on the second bar; strategies wait for their signals. Both liquidate at the final close.
  5. Defined limits. No funding, borrow costs, liquidity, liquidation, or intrabar drawdown model. Shorting is simplified. Replay reveals the completed run; it does not change its totals.

1 basis point (bp) = 0.01%. Settings are saved only in this browser. See the repository README for the accounting formulas.
